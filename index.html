Formatting Rules

  • Use ISO language codes for languages: EN, DE, FR, ES, PT, JP, KR (not "English", "German", etc.)
  • Use ISO country codes for countries: US, UK, DE, FR, CA, AU (not "USA", "United States", etc.)
  • Enter creator handles without @: mrbeast, ninja, pokimane (not @mrbeast)
  • Numbers should be entered without commas: 5000000 (not 5,000,000)

How to Use

  1. Download the template for your campaign platform
  2. Fill in the creator data (one row per creator)
  3. Save your file with a descriptive campaign name (e.g., "Summer2026_TwitchCampaign.csv")
  4. Go to the Campaign Report tab to upload and generate your report
